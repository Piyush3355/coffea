02 / ABOUT COFFEA

We Create The Delicious Coffee Memory You Love

Founded in 2012 in Seattle, Coffea was born out of a deep reverence for the farmers who cultivate exceptional cherries and a passion for architectural minimalism.

Every step—from evaluating green bean moisture at origin to profiling roast curves and calibrating water mineral content—is executed with obsessive culinary precision.

✓ Direct Trade transparency paying 340% above market minimums
✓ 100% organic, traceable micro-lot harvests with cupping scores 88+
✓ Certified master baristas trained in latte art and sensory cupping
✓ Custom dual-boiler PID machines with gentle 6-second pre-infusion
